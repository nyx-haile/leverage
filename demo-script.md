# Leverage Video Script

## 0:00-0:18 - Opener

Your life is scattered across systems.

Your calendar knows where you are supposed to be. Your email knows what people need from you. Your task list knows what you promised. Your health data knows how you slept. Your location history knows where you actually went. The news, your classes, your clubs, and the random things people ask of you all become part of the same hidden workload.

Every productivity app sees this and gives you the same solution: another visualizer.

A dashboard. A timeline. A prettier calendar. A new way to drag boxes around.

Somehow, this one is supposed to be different.

## 0:18-0:30 - Hook

But the problem is not the signals.

The problem is you.

Not because you are broken, but because you are inside the system you are trying to understand.

## 0:30-0:48 - Problem

When you are tired, you are the one who has to notice why you are tired.

When you are stressed, you are the one who has to debug your stress.

When you are behind, you are the one who has to figure out why you keep starting late.

Every productivity app gives you another view of the data. But you are still doing the science.

## 0:48-1:03 - Product

This is **Leverage**: a local-first N-of-1 research assistant for your personal life.

You choose the goals. You approve the data. You decide what experiments to run.

Leverage gives you the outside view.

## 1:03-1:18 - Persona

Meet Maya, an overloaded MIT student trying to succeed sustainably.

She has hard classes, psets, labs, club responsibilities, sleep debt, skipped meals, and weeks that look possible on a calendar but fall apart in real life.

## 1:18-1:34 - Mechanism

Leverage combines structured data, like email, calendar, tasks, deadlines, sleep, meals, health, location, and news, with semantic interviews.

The AI only extracts features from the interviews.

The rest is deterministic: correlations, evidence rows, schedule constraints, uncertainty, and experiment design.

## 1:34-1:49 - Demo Moment

Maya tells Leverage she keeps starting work late and crashing near deadlines.

Leverage processes the interview, compares it to her data, and surfaces a causal candidate:

> When you start psets less than 24 hours before they are due, your sleep drops, your completion rate falls, and the next day gets worse.

## 1:49-2:04 - Experiment

So Leverage proposes a small experiment:

Start the next pset 48 hours earlier.

Move the first work block out of the dorm.

Protect meals and sleep.

Take real breaks instead of pretending rest is optional.

Then measure whether the week improves.

## 2:04-2:12 - Safeguards + Close

All personal data stays on-device. You can delete or export it.

Leverage does not diagnose, treat, or act for you.

It helps you get enough distance from your own life to learn what actually helps you succeed.
